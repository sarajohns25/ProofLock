What does verification mean? ProofLock compares the uploaded file's SHA-256 fingerprint with the fingerprint recorded on the Sepolia blockchain. A match indicates that the uploaded file has the same contents as the file represented by that blockchain record. It does not prove who originally created the file or whether its contents are truthful.
